# Change proposal

Read this only when the user accepts the offer at the end of the assessment or asks for a change proposal. A proposal is not part of `assessment.json`.

Prepare it from the finished, self-checked assessment. Describe the exact volume/account/region, source-defined snapshot/deletion/archival proposal, estimated cost basis and unresolved prerequisites. Independently establish ownership and intended use, retention/compliance requirements, a recoverable snapshot/restore plan, snapshot/KMS access, infrastructure management and current attachment state. These are **change prerequisites**, not added finder validations. Metrics absence and `available` state alone do not establish owner intent or recovery readiness. Identify current-resource versus future-provisioning effects. Do not execute deletion, snapshot creation or archival through this assessment workflow. Explicitly authorized execution belongs to a separately reviewed, concrete operation with fresh state and recovery evidence.

**Steps the estimate models.** The after cost models one path: snapshot the volume, move that snapshot to the archive tier and keep it, billed at the full volume size for twelve months, then delete the volume. Propose those steps. A different path, such as no snapshot, a snapshot kept in the standard tier or a shorter retention, does not match the modeled after cost; say so and do not reuse the estimate for it. State the retention implications: the archive tier has a 90-day minimum, and deleting an archived snapshot earlier is charged for the rest of it; restoring an archived snapshot takes up to 72 hours before a volume can be created from it; and a deleted volume cannot be recovered without its snapshot.
